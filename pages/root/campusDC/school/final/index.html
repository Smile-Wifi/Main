About SMCCI

A Century of Excellence in Catholic Education

Founded in 1915 by Rev. Fr. Joseph Menken, MSC, Saint Michael College, Cantilan, Inc. is a private Catholic higher education institution dedicated to providing integral, quality, Catholic education.

Located in the quiet lush green municipality of Cantilan, Surigao del Sur, we stand as a lighthouse guiding the young to the safe shores of knowledge and faith, committed to proclaiming Jesus Christ as the Good News to all.

Our motto "Caritas Christi Urget Nos" (The Charity of Christ Impels Us) inspires us to develop competent, socially responsible professionals and lifelong learners who will serve the Church and society.
