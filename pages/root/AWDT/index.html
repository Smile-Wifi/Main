The 5-Stage Deep Ocean Process

Every 20 seconds: one 3m × 8m ice cylinder rises from 1,000m depth

1

Particle Barrier

Hydrostatic pressure filters seawater through self-cleaning barrier. No pumps.

2

UV Purification

Intense UV irradiation eliminates all biological contaminants in closed chamber.

3

Icing

3m × 8m ice cylinder formed every 20s using deep-sea cold. Buoyancy lifts it upward.

4

Cleansing

Salt water expelled via natural streams. No brine concentration.

5

Melting

Ice smashed and melted using recovered heat. Pure water ready for delivery.
